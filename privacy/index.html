GymNinja privacy policy

Last updated 27 September 2026

GymNinja is a free app that gyms use to run memberships, fees, attendance and personal training, and that their members use to see their plan and progress. This page explains what we store and why.

What we store

  • Account: your mobile number and a PIN or password (stored hashed by our database provider). If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google, nothing else.
  • Gym records entered by your gym: name, mobile number, plan, fees, payments (including UPI reference numbers you submit), check-in times, and personal-training plans and check-ins.
  • Location at check-in, only if your gym has turned on the check-in distance check. It is used once to confirm you are at the gym and is not stored as a history.
  • Your own log (workouts, diet, weight, body measurements, steps) is kept on your phone. A private backup copy is kept in your account that only you can read; your gym cannot see it.

Who can see it

  • Your gym's owner and staff see the records of their own gym only. Other gyms cannot see them.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or share it with anyone for marketing.
  • Data is stored with Supabase (database and file storage) in the Mumbai region. Payments go straight from you to your gym's UPI ID; GymNinja never handles money.

Messages

Reminders are sent by your gym through WhatsApp from their own phone. GymNinja does not send SMS or WhatsApp messages on its own.

Your choices

  • You can stop using location check-in and check in at the desk instead.
  • To have your account and data deleted, ask your gym, or write to us at the address below. We delete it within 30 days, except payment records your gym must keep by law.
