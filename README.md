# GymNinja

Free gym management app. Hosted build of the app lives here.

**Open on your phone:** https://shivamgiri1995.github.io/gymninja/

**Android app:** [GymNinja.apk](https://github.com/shivamgiri1995/gymninja/releases/latest/download/GymNinja.apk)
(allow "install from unknown sources" when Android asks)

- **Gym owners:** set up a gym in minutes, print one desk QR per branch, then see who is inside,
  footfall by hour, fees paid / pending / overdue, UPI payments to confirm, join requests,
  workout & diet PDFs with open counts, notices and monthly reports.
- **Members:** scan the desk QR to join and to check in / out, today's workout with every set logged,
  exercise library, Indian diet plan by goal with a food log, steps, weight / BMI (Asian scale),
  body measurements, PRs, monthly report, and fees by UPI.

Try it without an account: open the link and tap **"Just looking? Try a demo gym"**.

This repository only holds the compiled web app (branch `gh-pages`) and Android releases.
